## 📘 Contribution Guideline

### Purpose

This document serves as a blueprint for contributing chapters to the **AI Development Guidelines**. Each chapter should resemble a scientific computer science paper: precise, well-structured, and justified with real-world relevance or empirical insight. Additionally, we plan to make contributions to https://huggingface.co/SpaceDevUy, providing an opportunity for contributors to showcase their work and expertise as AI developers in this collaborative space.

### Introduction

When starting your contribution, consider these guiding questions to frame your chapter effectively:

* What specific problem or knowledge gap does your chapter address?
* How will this content help AI practitioners improve their work?
* What unique perspective or expertise are you bringing to this topic?
* How does this topic connect to the broader AI development ecosystem?
* What practical outcomes should readers expect after studying this chapter?

Answering these questions will help you create a focused, valuable contribution that meets the needs of our audience.

### General Expectations

* Follow a clear, logical structure.
* Use precise, technical language.
* Cite references, tools, or datasets where applicable.
* Include diagrams or examples when helpful.
* Ensure reproducibility for code or experiments, if included.

## ✍️ Required Chapter Structure

Each chapter **must** address the following sections and guiding questions:

### 1. **Motivation**

> Set the stage for why this chapter matters.

* What problem or opportunity does this chapter address? Describe the real-world need or technical gap.
* Why is this topic important in the context of AI development? Connect it to broader trends or industry needs.
* Who is the intended audience? Clarify the level of expertise you're writing for.

### 2. **Background and Context**

> Provide readers with the necessary foundation.

* What key concepts, prior work, or tools are required to understand this topic? Add citations or visual references when helpful.
* How does this relate to existing ML/AI workflows? Position it within existing methodologies or tools.

### 3. **Technical Description**

> Dive deep into the core methods or systems.

* What are the core ideas, algorithms, or techniques being introduced or discussed? Define and explain them.
* What are the formal definitions, assumptions, or parameters? Include relevant equations or logical conditions.
* How does this method work? Include pseudocode, flowcharts, or diagrams to clarify the process.

### 4. **Examples and Applications**

> Make it tangible with real-world context.

* Can you provide an example, case study, or a project at Space where something related was used? Add screenshots or references if possible.
* What code, data, or services are involved? Include links or brief usage explanations.
* Include reproducible snippets or links to repositories if applicable. Walk readers through a basic example.

### 5. **Evaluation and Comparison** (if relevant)

> Highlight the effectiveness, limitations, and alternatives.

* How does this technique compare to alternatives? Try to give options, the same technology isn't always applicable, but similar ones might be.
* What are its limitations and trade-offs? These might not be obvious during development, but reflecting on them helps future contributors understand your choices better.
* Include quantitative or qualitative assessments where possible. Metrics, benchmarks, or subjective reviews can work. If you're short on time, just include the links and resources that helped you decide.

### 6. **Best Practices and Recommendations**

> Help others succeed with your insights.

* What should practitioners keep in mind when using this? Explain considerations based on your real-world experience.
* Any pitfalls to avoid? Think of mistakes you made, or edge cases others should handle. This is where your lessons learned shine.

### 7. **Conclusion**

> Wrap it up with clarity and actionable insight.

* Summarize the key takeaways. Use bullet points – they're great for highlighting what matters.
* Suggest directions for further reading or exploration. Mention libraries, tutorials, papers, or videos that expand on your topic.

## 📄 Formatting and Style Guidelines

* Use [Markdown](https://www.markdownguide.org/basic-syntax/) format.
* Use LaTeX for equations if needed (`$$...$$`).
* Code blocks should be language-tagged (e.g., \`\`\`python).
* Figures and diagrams can be added via URLs or static images.

## ✅ Checklist Before Submission

* [ ] All required sections are included.
* [ ] Content has been proofread for grammar and clarity. (You could use Grammarly or similar tools)
* [ ] Technical content is accurate and reproducible. (Best plan: try to reproduce using ONLY what you wrote)
* [ ] Citations and references are added where appropriate.
* [ ] Create a Pull Request to the main branch so the team can review your article and provide feedback.
